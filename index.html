Legendary Notes

-If you need to put multiple classes in a card's colors and/or rules text, they are always ordered as such: :icon_class_Strength: , :icon_class_Instinct:, :icon_class_Covert:, :icon_class_Tech:, :icon_class_Ranged:. (Divided cards do not follow this rule relative to each of their "halves") -In rules text, with "Reveal the top card from _ deck" effects you'd return the revealed card back to the top of that deck after resolving all of that card's effects, without the need for more wording. However, the same effect for multiple cards must always specify what happens with all/the rest of the cards that are revealed. -In Legendary Language™, you "Gain" things with physical substance to them, like Hero and Wound cards, as well as Shards and Throne's Favor, which are meant to be represented with solid tokens. On the flipside, you "Get" resources are purely imaginary, such as :icon_legendary_currency_Attack: , :icon_legendary_currency_Recruit: and :icon_legendary_PiercingEnergy: -List of words and terms you should Capitalize on your cards: Card Types, like Hero, Villain, Bystander; Deck Types like Hero Deck, Villain Deck (Do not capitalize the word "deck" when it's isolated) Victory Pile and Escape Pile, but NOT KO pile or discard pile City Spaces (like Sewers, Bridge). Do not capitalize the words "city" or "city spaces." All Keywords. -Words and terms you should bold on your cards: Section Headers (like Fight:, Ambush:, Master Strike:, Evil Wins:, etc.) Keywords, and numbers/descriptors that are part of Keywords (like Focus 3 :icon_legendary_currency_Recruit: or Sewers Conqueror 1) Attack & Recruit Numbers within card text (like You get +1 :icon_legendary_currency_Attack: or You get +3 :icon_legendary_currency_Recruit:) -Most Villains follow a simple balance formula that tells how much Attack (:icon_legendary_currency_Attack:) they should have: Villain :icon_legendary_currency_Attack: = Villain :icon_legendary_VP: +2 -Keywords and special abilities can alter the "formula" slightly: Most abilities that range between +0-5 :icon_legendary_currency_Attack: are valued as if they were a flat +2 (e.g. Striker, Dark Memories, Empowered, etc.) Conditional abilities that give 0 :icon_legendary_currency_Attack:, -2 :icon_legendary_currency_Attack:, or +2 :icon_legendary_currency_Attack: are valued as equal to a flat 1 :icon_legendary_currency_Attack: on the formula. (e.g. Size-Changing.) Non-numeric effects that may prevent you from fighting a Villain normally (e.g. Chivalrous Duel) are sometimes valued as +2 :icon_legendary_currency_Attack: on the formula, but can vary depending on the difficulty of the requirement.
